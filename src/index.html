About Accuracy

Kairos chooses the most accurate measurement method based on your device type. On laptops, all system data is pulled from the battery management unit; on desktop systems, CPU and GPU sensor data are complemented with estimated consumption of motherboards and other components to provide a projection. These values are approximately 95% accurate to actual measurements.
